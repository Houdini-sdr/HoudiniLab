(1) **It IS the sensor** -- the steering loop's error signal is eps, and the tracker is the only thing that measures it, so removing it removes the actuator's input. (2) **Steering fixes RATE, never OFFSET** -- propagation delay, cable and RF-chain latency are a static time offset that no frequency correction touches; the UE still has to know where the BS frame starts, which is what `houdini_pilot_ref` is. LTE keeps timing advance on perfectly frequency-locked networks for exactly this reason. (3) **Bandwidth** -- the steer is deliberately slow (30-60 s, and slower still for AP-51), so re-acquisition, beacon glitches and post-escalation resync are all inner-loop work; the clock cannot respond within a frame.
